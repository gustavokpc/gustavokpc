# Gustavo Kuratomi

Electrical Engineering student at the University of São Paulo (Poli-USP), currently pursuing a double degree in Robotics at ENSTA Paris.

My work focuses on robotics and machine learning, with experience in autonomous systems, reinforcement learning, and natural language processing.

Previously, I worked on learning-based quadrotor control at ONERA and ISAE-SUPAERO, combining neural networks, simulation, and experimental validation on real drones.

I also conducted research at the University of São Paulo on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for question-answering over institutional regulations, resulting in a publication at ENIAC 2024.
